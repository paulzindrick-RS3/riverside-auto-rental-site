Serving Sparta, Fort McCoy & the Elroy-Sparta Trail

Sparta is the Bicycling Capital of America, at the junction of the Elroy-Sparta State Trail and the La Crosse River State Trail. The Elroy-Sparta trail runs 32.5 miles and is considered the first rail-to-trail in the United States. The Wisconsin DNR reports as many as 60,000 visitors a year. Trail groups use the 15-passenger van to shuttle riders and gear.

Butterfest is held every June in Sparta, with a parade, carnival, and a busy weekend in town. The van goes quickly that weekend, so call when your dates are set.

Fort McCoy visitors and families can rent from us too. The Deke Slayton Memorial Space & Bicycle Museum and the downtown four-corners district bring visitors who sometimes need a car while they are in the area. If a Sparta-area vehicle is in a body shop, call about an insurance replacement.
