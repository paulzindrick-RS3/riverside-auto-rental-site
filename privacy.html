Privacy

What this website collects, and how to ask a privacy question.

Riverside Auto Rental is at N5856 WI-54, Black River Falls, WI 54615. This page describes the contact form and the Riverside Rental Help chat on this website.

This website uses Google Analytics to understand how visitors use the site.

Contact form

The contact page has a form that opens the email program on your device, addressed to contact@riversideautorental.com. The fields are your name, email address, phone number, a topic, and a message. This website does not store that form on a server.

Reaching a person

To reach a person, text or call 715-396-1466. That text or call goes to the business directly, not through this website.

Riverside Rental Help chat

Riverside Rental Help is an automated chat assistant. It is not a person. It answers questions about our rates, vehicles, hours and policies, and it can take down a rental request or a message for our team.

The chat is run by Retell, an outside service. What you type in the chat, including any name, phone number or trip details you share, is sent to Retell to write the replies. Retell keeps a record of the conversation, and our team can read it so we can call you back.

How long Retell keeps chat records is set by Retell, not by this website. See Retell's privacy policy.

The chat cannot confirm that a vehicle is available or book a rental. A team member confirms every request. For an emergency with a rental, call 715-396-1466.

Sensitive numbers

Don't type a driver's license number, a payment card number, a Social Security number, or other sensitive numbers into the chat or the contact form.

Privacy questions

For a privacy question, call or text 715-396-1466 or email contact@riversideautorental.com.
