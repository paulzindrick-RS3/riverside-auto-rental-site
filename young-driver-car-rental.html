Age

Can I Rent a Car at 21?

Yes. Riverside rents to drivers 21 and older. There's no extra fee for drivers 21 to 24. You pay the same published rates as everyone else, and there are no vehicle limits at 21. You can rent any vehicle in the fleet, including the 15-passenger van and the 7-passenger Chrysler Pacifica.

Many national rental companies add a daily young-renter fee for drivers under 25, and some limit which vehicles under-25 renters can take, such as minivans and passenger vans. Riverside does not add that fee, and drivers 21 to 24 can take any vehicle we have.

Under 21

What If I'm Under 21?

We can't rent to anyone under 21. If you need a car and you're not 21 yet, a parent or other adult who is 21 or older can rent the vehicle and drive it. Questions about adding other drivers? Call 715-396-1466 for details. Every person who drives has to be listed on the rental agreement.

Requirements

What You Need to Rent at 21–24

The requirements are the same for every renter, including drivers 21 to 24.

Valid U.S. driver's license

Bring a current U.S. driver's license. It cannot be expired or suspended.

Proof of full-coverage auto insurance

Bring proof of a full-coverage policy that covers you. A digital copy on your phone is fine. Riverside doesn't sell insurance, so sort out coverage before you book. Questions about using a parent's policy? Call 715-396-1466 for details.

Security deposit

A security deposit, starting at $300, is held separately from your rental charges on a credit or debit card (Visa, Mastercard, or Discover; no Amex). We'll confirm the exact amount when you book. We do not accept checks. Questions about whose name must be on the card? Call 715-396-1466 for details. Drivers 21 to 24 pay the same deposit terms as every other renter.

Every driver listed

Every person who will drive must be listed on the rental. Questions about who can be added? Call 715-396-1466 for details.

More on the paperwork is in the first-time renter's guide and in deposits and debit cards.
