Requirements

What You Need to Rent the Van

Be 21 or older. No young-driver fee, and renters 21 to 24 can drive the van. See renting at 21 to 24, no young-driver fee. Bring a valid U.S. driver's license (no special license for personal use) and proof of full-coverage auto insurance. Riverside doesn't sell insurance. Check with your carrier that your policy covers a 15-passenger van.

A security deposit, starting at $300, is held separately from your rental charges on a credit or debit card (Visa, Mastercard, or Discover; no Amex). We'll confirm the exact amount when you book. All drivers must be listed. Added-driver age: call 715-396-1466 for details. No pets, no smoking, return the van full, and give 24-hour cancellation notice. After-hours drop-off returns are available.

Read the first-timer guide and the deposit and debit card guide. A 15-passenger van is available when the claim involves a larger group or family vehicle.
