Fall Color Road Trips from Black River Falls: Routes and the Right Rental

Peak color runs late September through mid-October out here. Five drives that start within two hours of our lot — and the vehicle to take on each.

Western Wisconsin doesn't have to work hard to earn a fall color reputation — the sandstone bluffs, river valleys, and hardwood ridges around Black River Falls do it every year. Most seasons the show starts in late September and peaks in the first two weeks of October, river valleys holding on a little longer.

Here are five drives we'd send anyone on, closest first.

The five drives

  • Black River State Forest: minutes from town · 68,000 acres of oak and pine, castle-mound overlooks off Highway 54 and Smrekar Road
  • Mill Bluff State Park (Camp Douglas): ~35 minutes · prairie-island bluffs rising straight out of the flats, quick stairway hikes to the views
  • Great River Road (WI-35), Trempealeau & Perrot State Park: ~50 minutes to the river · the Mississippi backed by 500-foot bluffs in full color
  • Kickapoo Valley & Wildcat Mountain State Park (Ontario): ~1 hour 15 · winding ridge roads, Amish farm country around Cashton on the way
  • The full Great River Road loop (La Crosse → Fountain City → back): an all-day, 150–200 mile circle that earns every mile

Match the vehicle to the drive

Couple, camera, thermos: an intermediate car ($55/day local) is all you need for the paved routes — and the cheapest seat for the show.

Forest roads and overlook pull-offs: the Ford Edge SUV or AWD Subaru Outback (from $80/day) rides taller — a real advantage for sightlines over the guardrails — and shrugs off the gravel spurs in the state forest.

Three generations and a picnic: the 7-passenger minivan ($78/day + $0.10/mile) takes grandparents, kids, folding chairs, and the cooler — everyone looking at the same trees at the same time.

The mileage tier is trip-dependent

Black River State Forest and Mill Bluff days stay well inside the local tier (100 miles/day on cars and SUVs). The river-road and Kickapoo drives run 120–200 miles — take the unlimited daily tier ($75 intermediate / $85 full-size / $100 SUV) and chase every spur road that looks promising, because the best overlook is always one more turn away.

Planning a full color week — a cabin stay with day drives? Rentals of 7+ days include unlimited mileage automatically; the minivan runs $450/week, cars from $450/week unlimited.

October weekends fill from two sides

Fall color season overlaps wedding season and hunting-camp setup, and October Saturdays quietly become some of the busiest non-holiday days of the fall. A week or two of lead time is usually enough — but for a peak-color Saturday, call as soon as the forecast firms up.
