Jackson County Fair 2026: The Right Rental for Fair Week

July 28 – August 2 at Jackson County Fair Park. Vans for groups, cars for visiting family — and why the two weeks before the fair are when to call.

The Jackson County Fair comes home to Black River Falls Tuesday, July 28 through Sunday, August 2, 2026, at Jackson County Fair Park on Melrose Street. For six days, it's the biggest thing happening in the county — livestock shows, grandstand events, food stands, carnival rides, and half the county in one place.

We're about five minutes from the fairgrounds, and every year fair week shows up in our reservation book in two ways: groups that need a van, and out-of-town family that needs a car. Here's how to handle both without a last-minute scramble.

Groups: one van beats a convoy

4-H clubs hauling kids and project gear, church youth groups making a day of it, extended families coming in from three directions — fair trips are group trips. A convoy of four cars means four parking spots, four sets of keys, and somebody always lost on the way in.

Our 15-passenger van ($135/day + $0.10/mile) puts the whole group in one vehicle with room left over for coolers and lawn chairs. For smaller crews, the 7-passenger minivan ($78/day + $0.10/mile) does the same job at a lower rate — and the sliding doors make loading kids in a gravel lot painless.

Because the fairgrounds are in town, a fair-day van rental barely touches the mileage meter. A full day of hauling your group to and from the fair might run 30–40 miles total — $3 or $4 in mileage on top of the daily rate.

Visiting family: a car waiting when they arrive

Fair week doubles as reunion week for a lot of Jackson County families. If relatives are flying into La Crosse or Eau Claire, or driving up and leaving a vehicle behind, a rental car solves the "who's picking them up, and how do they get around all week" problem.

Intermediate cars start at $55/day local (100 mi/day, plenty for fair week around town) and full-size cars at $65/day local. Staying a full week? The weekly local rates ($330 intermediate / $390 full-size) beat seven daily rentals — our weekly rates guide walks through the math.

Why now is the time to call

The fair starts two weeks from today. Vans are the pinch point — we keep a limited number of 15-passenger vans, they're one of the only large passenger vans available in the area, and summer weekends already keep them moving. When fair week lands on top of normal summer demand, the calendar fills.

The fix is simple: call or text 715-396-1466 as soon as your plans firm up. Tell us the dates, the head count, and whether the trip stays local. Reserving takes a few minutes and there's no prepayment gymnastics — we're a local, independent shop with flat published rates.
