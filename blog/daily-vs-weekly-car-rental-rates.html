Daily vs. Weekly Car Rental Rates: Which Saves You More?

A straightforward breakdown of our pricing options — so you pick the one that fits your trip and budget.

One of the most common questions we get at Riverside Auto Rental — the independent, family-owned car rental in Black River Falls since 1983 — is: "Should I rent by the day or by the week?" The answer depends on how long you need the vehicle and how far you're driving. Here's the full breakdown.

Understanding our daily rates

Local renters get unlimited mileage on the published local daily rates. Non-local rates are intermediate $75/day or $450/week, full-size $85/day or $510/week, and SUV/AWD $100/day or $600/week, plus a per-mile charge. Call 715-396-1466 for the per-mile rate. Local means residents of Black River Falls and Jackson County, WI.

Weekly rates — where the real savings kick in

If you need a vehicle for 7 days or more, the weekly rate is almost always the better deal. You pay for 6 days and get the 7th free. Local weekly rates include unlimited mileage. Non-local rates are intermediate $75/day or $450/week, full-size $85/day or $510/week, and SUV/AWD $100/day or $600/week, plus a per-mile charge. Call 715-396-1466 for the per-mile rate.
