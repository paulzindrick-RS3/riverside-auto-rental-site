Requirements

What You Need to Rent

  • You must be 21 or older. Renting at 21 to 24, no young-driver fee.
  • A valid U.S. driver's license.
  • Proof of full-coverage auto insurance. A digital copy is fine. Riverside doesn't sell insurance.
  • A security deposit, starting at $300, is held separately from your rental charges on a credit or debit card (Visa, Mastercard, or Discover; no Amex). We'll confirm the exact amount when you book. We do not accept checks.
  • Every driver must be listed on the rental.
  • No pets. A cleaning charge applies.
  • Non-smoking. A $100 cleaning charge applies if someone smokes in the vehicle.
  • Return the car with a full tank.
  • Cancellation needs 24 hours' notice.
  • After-hours drop-off returns are available. Call 715-396-1466 for details.
