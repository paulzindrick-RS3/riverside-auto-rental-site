Why Rent an SUV Here

When Western Wisconsin Calls for More Than a Sedan

Winter Driving on I-94 and County Roads

Lake-effect snow, black ice, and drifting on open stretches between Black River Falls, Tomah, and Osseo. AWD and higher ground clearance make the difference from November through March.

Gravel Access to Hunting Land

Gun deer season fills Jackson County every November. An SUV handles the gravel and dirt access roads to hunting land and cabins around the Black River State Forest without worry.

Trail & Forest Visitors

Heading to the Black River State Forest, area campgrounds, or ATV trailheads? Extra cargo room swallows gear, coolers, and muddy boots that a sedan trunk can't.

Insurance Replacement Upgrades

If your SUV or truck is in the body shop, ask your adjuster about a comparable replacement. We work directly with adjusters and shops across western Wisconsin.
